# 🐄 The Calf Nerd — Your Calf Health Command Center

Welcome to **The Calf Nerd Network and Resource Hub**! A comprehensive platform dedicated to evidence-based calf health management, nutrition calculators, and community support for dairy farmers and calf raisers.

---

## 🎯 Mission

**Give every calf raiser access to the tools, data, and community they need — so no one has to guess.**

Raising calves shouldn't mean guessing. The Calf Nerd provides tools built on university research, NASEM standards, and field-tested protocols—all in one accessible hub.

---

## ✨ What We Offer

### 🧮 Nutrition Calculators
- Milk replacer mixing and formulation
- Whole milk feeding calculations
- Colostrum quality assessment (Brix → IgG)
- Feeding rate recommendations based on NASEM standards

### 📊 Growth Tracking
- Monitor average daily gain (ADG)
- Compare feeding programs side-by-side
- Benchmark against industry standards
- Track individual and herd performance metrics

### 📋 Health Protocols
- Evidence-based protocols for scours management
- Respiratory health guidelines
- Navel care best practices
- Biosecurity protocols ready for your barn wall

### 📚 Resource Library
- Curated articles from leading universities
- Extension publications and research summaries
- Industry expert insights
- Searchable, always-updated resource database

### 👥 Calf Nerd Network
- Join a growing community of calf raisers
- Share knowledge and troubleshoot problems
- Access real-world solutions and best practices
- Raise the standard together
